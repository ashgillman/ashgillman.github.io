I am a Radiation Oncology Medical Physics (ROMP) registrar at Townsville Hospital and Health Service, where I am training in the clinical application of physics to deliver safe, accurate and effective radiotherapy.
I am also an adjunct research staff at James Cook University (JCU).

Previously, as a research scientist at CSIRO, I developed and validated quantitative imaging biomarkers across PET, MRI and nuclear medicine. My work included PET motion correction, harmonising biomarkers across clinical sites, multimodal image registration, and AI-assisted motion analysis in MRI-guided radiotherapy. I contributed to major collaborative initiatives including the Australian Imaging, Biomarkers and Lifestyle study and the Australian Dementia Network.

On the weekends, you'll find me riding bikes with my two boys, or fermenting beer, sourdough, kombucha or pickles.
